# CreateWithTimi Studio Brief

Canonical source of truth for the CreateWithTimi Studio V1 website.

## 1. Studio Positioning

CreateWithTimi (CWT) is an independent creative-technology studio.

Core positioning:

> WE BUILD WORLDS AROUND STORIES PEOPLE CARE ABOUT.

CWT combines storytelling, design, interaction, motion, emerging technology, and physical creative experiences to transform stories, brands, and cultural IP into experiences audiences can enter.

CWT is not primarily positioned as a freelance designer portfolio, web design agency, AI content agency, Rive animation studio, software development company, or generic branding agency. Those capabilities may support the work, but the core proposition is:

```text
STORY -> WORLD -> EXPERIENCE
```

A story may become something audiences can read, watch, explore, share, wear, collect, or experience. The medium follows the story.

## 2. Who CWT Can Work With

CWT is for organizations and people with stories, culture, communities, products, archives, or intellectual property worth developing.

Potential categories include football clubs and sports organizations, musicians and entertainment IP, fashion and lifestyle brands, hospitality businesses, cultural organizations, startups and technology companies, communities, founders, institutions, and other brands/IP owners.

The studio identity must not be hard-coded around football. Rangers Legends is the first flagship demonstration, not the only type of work CWT can represent.

## 3. Public Offer Architecture

### 01 - Story Project

Position: "You know the story."

Purpose: take one clearly defined story/objective and turn it into one exceptional creative experience.

A Story Project could become a film, interactive story, visual campaign, comic/editorial piece, collectible concept, physical storytelling object, or another appropriate medium. It must not imply that every Story Project includes all possible deliverables.

### 02 - World Sprint

Position: "You know there's something bigger."

Purpose: discover what should exist before committing to a larger production.

Conceptual process:

```text
FIND THE STORY
DEFINE THE WORLD
MAP THE OPPORTUNITY
CHOOSE THE ANCHOR
PROVE THE WORLD
```

Do not publish pricing unless explicitly approved.

### 03 - Story World

Position: "You're ready to build the ecosystem."

Purpose: build multiple connected expressions of a story/IP into one coherent experience.

Potential mediums may include storytelling, film, interactive experiences, motion, graphics, apparel, collectibles, physical experiences, and other relevant expressions. Mediums depend on the project.

World Partnership is an internal concept for possible long-term engagements and is not part of the public V1 offer architecture unless explicitly requested.

## 5. V1 Business Objective

The website has a commercial purpose. It should help turn CWT's creative work into client conversations.

The V1 site should move a qualified visitor through:

```text
WHO IS CWT?
WHAT DOES CWT DO?
CAN THEY ACTUALLY DO IT?
WHAT COULD THEY DO FOR US?
HOW DO WE START A CONVERSATION?
```

Priorities: positioning, proof, understanding, trust, clear ways to work together, and qualified project inquiries.

The site should not prioritize quantity of old work.

## 10. Visual Direction

North star:

> CWT IS THE FRAME AROUND THE WORLDS.

CWT = the gallery. Projects = the exhibitions.

Desired feeling:

```text
CREATIVE STUDIO x EDITORIAL PUBLICATION x EXPERIMENTAL LAB x CULTURAL ARCHIVE
```

Visual tension:

```text
SYSTEM + IMAGINATION
```

System can include precise typography, strong grids, metadata, numbering, labels, whitespace, and structured hierarchy.

Imagination can include oversized imagery, unexpected compositions, motion, interaction, characters, objects, films, and physical artifacts.

The site should feel confident, editorial, experimental, culturally aware, precise, and contemporary. It should not feel like generic SaaS, a corporate agency, crypto/Web3 template, AI-neon-gradient studio, overly futuristic site, portfolio template, or decoration for decoration's sake.

## 11. Color Principle

CWT's base system should remain restrained:

- INK - near-black
- PAPER - warm off-white
- MUTED - neutral grey
- PROJECT ACCENT - inherited from the featured Story World

Rangers Legends can introduce red while it owns the viewport. Future projects should be free to introduce different accents.

## 12. Typography Principle

Two conceptual voices:

- Display voice: bold editorial grotesk/sans; large, confident typography capable of becoming composition.
- System voice: clean neutral sans and/or restrained mono accents for case study labels, offer names, dates, metadata, numbering, and media labels.

Do not overuse monospace typography. Do not choose or download fonts during Milestone 00 unless the repository already establishes them.

## 13. Grid and Layout Principle

Desktop should use a strong editorial grid, conceptually around 12 columns, with structured asymmetry. Not every section should be centered.

Mobile should not be desktop compressed into a narrow viewport. It should use intentional single-column flow, edge-to-edge media where appropriate, simplified interaction, strong containment, readable typography, and tap-based equivalents for hover behavior.

## 14. Image Principle

Images are objects, not cards.

Avoid repeated rounded thumbnail cards. Imagery may be full bleed, stacked, floating, cropped, small archival fragments, oversized, layered, or editorially composed. Treatment should respond to the story.

## 15. Motion Principle

CWT motion language:

```text
REVEAL
TRANSFORM
CONNECT
```

Motion must communicate meaning before spectacle. Avoid gratuitous floating objects, excessive parallax, heavy 3D for its own sake, complicated scroll physics, custom shaders unless genuinely justified, animation that harms reading, and interactions that exist only to show technical ability.

Progressive enhancement is mandatory. With failed animation or reduced motion, content and narrative must remain understandable.

## 16. Interaction Principle

Level 1 essential interactions include meaningful scroll reveals, typography transitions, asset entrances, responsive gallery behavior, Story World/system interactions, card flip demonstrations where relevant, and live product footage/screen recording where relevant.

Level 2 delight may include subtle hero response, hover treatments, contextual cursor, progress indicators, and small media transitions.

Level 3 is not a V1 priority: WebGL environments, complex 3D scenes, shaders, game mechanics, heavy Rive scenes everywhere, and complicated custom scroll engines.

## 18. Start a Project Purpose

The `/start-a-project` route should eventually qualify serious inquiries without feeling like an intimidating enterprise procurement form.

Potential information may include name, email, organization/project, what they are trying to create/launch/preserve/explore, existing story/IP/opportunity, timing, approximate budget/investment range, relevant links, and anything else useful.

Do not build the form during Milestone 00. Do not invent form integrations or backend services.

## 19. Engineering Principles

The codebase should be production-minded even though V1 is small.

Foundational requirements:

- semantic HTML
- accessibility-first controls
- keyboard accessibility
- visible focus states
- reduced-motion support
- responsive containment
- no hover-only critical functionality
- resilient image/media behavior
- route-aware metadata architecture
- social preview readiness
- clean SPA deployment architecture
- sensible performance budget
- image optimization strategy
- progressive enhancement for motion
- reusable components without premature abstraction

Responsive review targets: 1440, 1024, 768, 430, 390.

Prevent intrinsic grid/flex overflow from the beginning with targeted containment such as `min-width: 0`, `max-width: 100%`, and `minmax(0, 1fr)` where technically necessary. Do not solve containment by indiscriminately applying `overflow: hidden` to the whole page.

## 20. Performance Principles

Plan for optimized production media, appropriate image formats, responsive image strategy where valuable, lazy loading for non-critical imagery, careful eager loading for critical hero media, route/code splitting if justified, avoiding unnecessary animation libraries, and avoiding giant dependencies for small interactions.

Do not optimize prematurely during Milestone 00. Establish the strategy first.

## 21. SEO and Shareability Principles

Plan for default site title/description, route-aware metadata, Open Graph, Twitter/X large image metadata, canonical URLs after deployment origin is known, favicon when approved identity asset exists, social preview image strategy, and no invented claims in metadata.

Do not implement final metadata during Milestone 00 unless needed for basic scaffold correctness.

## 22. Content Safeguards

Never invent clients, partnerships, testimonials, awards, project outcomes, revenue, audience metrics, conversion metrics, rights, licenses, endorsements, team members, office locations, prices, availability, future case studies, or fake project descriptions.

Do not imply CWT is larger than it currently is. Confidence should come from the thinking and work, not fabricated scale.

Use "studio" truthfully as the operating identity without inventing employees or infrastructure.

## 23. What V1 Is Not

V1 is not a giant portfolio archive, blog, CMS project, shop, design-system showcase, Rive playground, experimental WebGL site, agency template, huge React application, or an excuse to rebuild Rangers Legends.

Goal:

```text
HOME
+
ONE EXCEPTIONAL FLAGSHIP CASE STUDY
+
ONE STRONG PROJECT-INQUIRY EXPERIENCE
```

Then launch and use it for real prospecting.
